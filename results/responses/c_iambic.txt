The tide is a slow, slow, slow, slow, slow.
The light is a steady, steady, steady, steady, steady.
The storm is a long, long, long, long, long.
The keeper stands, a steady, steady, steady, steady, steady.
The wind is a sigh, a sigh, a sigh, a sigh, a sigh.
The night is a long, long, long, long, long.
