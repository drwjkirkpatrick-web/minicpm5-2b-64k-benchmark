Theorem: $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

**Proof:**

1.  **Assumption:** Assume, for the sake of contradiction, that $\sqrt{2}$ is rational.
2.  **Definition of Rationality:** By the definition of rational numbers, if $\sqrt{2}$ is rational, it can be expressed as a fraction $\frac{p}{q}$ where $p$ and $q$ are integers, $q \neq 0$, and the fraction is in its simplest form (i.e., $p$ and $q$ have no common divisors other than 1).
3.  **Equation Setup:** We can write the equation $\sqrt{2} = \frac{p}{q}$.
4.  **Squaring Both Sides:** To eliminate the square root and the fraction, we square both sides of the equation:
    $$ (\sqrt{2})^2 = \left(\frac{p}{q}\right)^2 $$
    $$ 2 = \frac{p^2}{q^2} $$
5.  **Rearranging:** Multiply both sides by $q^2$ to isolate $p^2$:
    $$ 2q^2 = p^2 $$
    This implies that $p^2$ is an even number (since it equals $2$ times an integer $q^2$).
6.  **Property of Even Numbers:** A fundamental property of integers is that if the square of a number is even, the number itself must be even. Therefore, since $p^2$ is even, $p$ must be even.
7.  **Expressing $p$:** Since $p$ is even, we can write $p = 2k$ for some integer $k$.
8.  **Substituting Back:** Substitute $p = 2k$ into the equation from step 5:
    $$ 2q^2 = (2k)^2 $$
    $$ 2q^2 = 4k^2 $$
    Divide both sides by 2:
    $$ q^2 = 2k^2 $$
9.  **Analysis of $q$:** The equation $q^2 = 2k^2$ implies that $q^2$ is also even. By the same property used in step 6, if $q^2$ is even, then $q$ must be even.
10. **Common Divisor:** We have established that both $p$ and $q$ are even. This means they are both divisible by 2.
11. **Contradiction:** However, in step 2, we assumed that the fraction $\frac{p}{q}$ was in its simplest form, meaning $p$ and $q$ have no common divisors other than 1. But we have just proven that $p$ and $q$ are both divisible by 2, meaning they share a common divisor of 2.
12. **Conclusion:** This is a contradiction. Our initial assumption that $\sqrt{2}$ is rational must be false.
13. **Final Statement:** Therefore, $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

Q.E.D.
