To find the sum of all integers from 1 to 100, we can use the formula for the sum of the first $n$ positive integers:

$$S_n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

Here, $n = 100$. Plugging this value into the formula:

1.  Calculate $n(n+1)$:
    $$100 \times (100 + 1) = 100 \times 101 = 10100$$

2.  Divide by 2:
    $$\frac{10100}{2} = 5050$$

**Answer:**
The sum of all integers from 1 to 100 is **5,050**.
