This is a classic riddle that often leads to the wrong answer. Let's solve it step-by-step.

Let $B$ = cost of the ball and $Bat$ = cost of the bat.

We have two equations:
1. $B + Bat = 1.10$
2. $Bat = B + 1.00$

Substitute equation 2 into equation 1:
$B + (B + 1.00) = 1.10$
$2B + 1.00 = 1.10$
$2B = 0.10$
$B = 0.05$

So the ball costs **$0.05**.

The bat costs $0.05 + $1.00 = $1.05.

**Answer: The ball costs $0.05.**

*(The common mistake is to think the ball costs $0.10 — but that would make the bat $0.00, which doesn't work.)*
