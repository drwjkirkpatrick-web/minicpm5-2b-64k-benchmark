This is a classic lateral thinking puzzle. The trick is realizing that pianos aren't typically tuned in cities, but rather in homes. So the number of pianos (and thus tuners) in a city of 1 million people is effectively **zero**.

The reasoning:
- Most people don't have pianos.
- Even if they did, tuners are rarely needed in public spaces.
- The answer is a play on the assumption that you need a tuner for every piano in the city.

The joke answer is **0**.
